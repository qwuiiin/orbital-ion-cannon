Version: 4.0.0
Date: 2026-10-06
  Major Features:
    - Rewritten from scratch for Factorio 2.0 on top of Supercheese's Orbital Ion Cannon 1.8.2 (MIT). The mod is now MIT licensed.
    - Cannons orbit the planet they were launched from and fire only there (Space Exploration planets and moons, Space Age planets).
    - The targeting device works like an artillery remote: from the map, remote view or the ground.
  Changes:
    - No library dependencies.
    - Space Exploration cargo rockets deliver ion cannons as items; deploy them from a rocket silo.
    - Bob's mods recipe tweaks were dropped.
    - Saves from 3.x start with an empty orbit; saves from Orbital Ion Cannon 1.x keep their cannons.
